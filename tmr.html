Overview

The Maze Runner follows Thomas, who awakens in a mysterious place known as the Glade with no memory of his past. Alongside a group of other boys trapped inside a massive, ever-changing maze, he must uncover its secrets and find a way to escape before time runs out.

Trivia

  • The film is based on James Dashner's bestselling novel.
  • Most of the maze was created using a mix of practical sets and CGI.
  • It launched a successful trilogy of Maze Runner films.
