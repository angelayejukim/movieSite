Overview

Candy Jar follows Bennett Russell and Lona Skinner, two ambitious high school debate champions who are fierce academic rivals. Despite their constant competition and clashing personalities, they must work together to achieve their shared goal of getting into their dream colleges. Along the way, they discover unexpected friendship and romance.
