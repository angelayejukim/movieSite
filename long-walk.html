Overview

The Long Walk is set in a dystopian future where 50 teenage boys participate in a brutal walking contest. They must maintain a minimum speed while walking nonstop. If they slow down or stop too many times, they are eliminated from the competition in deadly fashion.

Trivia

  • The novel was written by Stephen King under the pen name Richard Bachman.
  • It was one of Stephen King's earliest stories.
