Overview

Sinners follows twin brothers Smoke and Stack as they return to their hometown hoping to leave their troubled past behind. Their fresh start quickly descends into terror when they encounter a sinister supernatural force that transforms their homecoming into a desperate fight for survival.

Trivia

  • The film reunites director Ryan Coogler with longtime collaborator Michael B. Jordan.
  • Michael B. Jordan plays dual roles as the twin brothers Smoke and Stack.
