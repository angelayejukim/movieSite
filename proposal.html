Overview

The Proposal follows Margaret Tate, a demanding book editor facing deportation from the United States, who convinces her assistant Andrew Paxton to pretend to be her fiancé. As they travel to his family home in Alaska to maintain the charade, unexpected romance begins to blossom.

Trivia

  • The film was a major box office success, grossing over $300 million worldwide.
  • Betty White's comedic performance became one of the film's highlights.
  • The movie was filmed primarily in Massachusetts, standing in for Alaska.
