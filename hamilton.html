Overview

Hamilton tells the story of American Founding Father Alexander Hamilton through a groundbreaking blend of hip-hop, R&B, pop, and traditional musical theatre. The filmed stage production captures the original Broadway cast performing the award-winning musical.

Trivia

  • The musical was created by Lin-Manuel Miranda.
  • It won 11 Tony Awards, including Best Musical.
  • The Disney+ film features the original Broadway cast.
