Overview

The teenage children of Disney's most infamous villains leave the Isle of the Lost to attend Auradon Prep. As they adjust to a new life, Mal, Evie, Carlos, and Jay must decide whether to follow in their parents' footsteps or forge their own paths.

Trivia

  • The film premiered on Disney Channel in 2015.
  • It launched a successful franchise with sequels, animated specials, and music albums.
  • Cameron Boyce's performance as Carlos became one of his most beloved roles.
