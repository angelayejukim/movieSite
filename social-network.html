Overview

The Social Network tells the story of how Mark Zuckerberg created Facebook while studying at Harvard University. As the website rapidly grows into a global social media platform, Zuckerberg faces lawsuits and personal conflicts with former friends and business partners.

Trivia

  • The screenplay was written by Aaron Sorkin and won the Academy Award for Best Adapted Screenplay.
  • The film's soundtrack by Trent Reznor and Atticus Ross won the Academy Award for Best Original Score.
