Overview

Casablanca follows American expatriate Rick Blaine, the owner of a nightclub in wartime Morocco, whose life is upended when his former lover Ilsa unexpectedly reappears with her husband, resistance leader Victor Laszlo. Forced to choose between love and sacrifice, Rick must decide where his loyalties truly lie.

Trivia

  • The film won three Academy Awards, including Best Picture, Best Director, and Best Adapted Screenplay.
  • Iconic lines such as "Here's looking at you, kid" and "We'll always have Paris" have become some of the most famous quotes in cinema history.
