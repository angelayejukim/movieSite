Overview

Lucy Hutton and Joshua Templeman are executive assistants working at the same publishing company. Their intense workplace rivalry turns into unexpected attraction as promotions, personal ambitions, and hidden feelings blur the line between love and hate.

Trivia

  • The film is based on Sally Thorne's bestselling 2016 novel.
  • Lucy Hale was a longtime fan of the book before being cast.
  • The enemies-to-lovers romance made it a favorite among rom-com fans.
