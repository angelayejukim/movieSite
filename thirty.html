Overview

13 Going on 30 follows Jenna Rink, a 13-year-old girl who wishes she could be thirty and successful. After making a birthday wish, she magically wakes up as her 30-year-old self living in New York City. While enjoying her new adult life, Jenna realizes that growing up isn't exactly what she imagined and learns what truly matters.

Trivia
