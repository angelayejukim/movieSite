Overview

The Princess Diaries follows shy teenager Mia Thermopolis, who discovers she is the heir to the throne of the European kingdom of Genovia. Guided by her elegant grandmother, Queen Clarisse, Mia must learn what it truly means to become a princess while staying true to herself.

Trivia

  • The film is based on Meg Cabot's bestselling novel.
  • It marked Anne Hathaway's feature film debut.
  • The movie became a beloved Disney classic and inspired a sequel.
