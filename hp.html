Overview

Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone follows eleven-year-old Harry Potter as he discovers that he is a wizard and begins his first year at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. Alongside his new friends Ron and Hermione, Harry uncovers the mystery surrounding the legendary Philosopher's Stone while confronting the dark forces connected to his past.

Trivia

  • The film is based on J.K. Rowling's bestselling debut novel of the same name.
  • It was the first installment in the Harry Potter film series and introduced audiences to Hogwarts, Diagon Alley, and many of the franchise's iconic characters.
