Overview

Easy A follows Olive Penderghast, a high school student whose little white lie about her dating life quickly spreads through school. As rumors grow, Olive uses her new reputation to help her classmates while learning valuable lessons about honesty, friendship, and staying true to herself.

Trivia

  • The film is loosely inspired by the novel The Scarlet Letter.
  • Emma Stone received a Golden Globe nomination for her performance.
  • Easy A became one of the most popular teen comedy films of the 2010s.
