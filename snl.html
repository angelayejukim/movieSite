Overview

Saturday Night is set during the 90 minutes leading up to the first broadcast of Saturday Night Live in 1975. The film follows producer Lorne Michaels and his cast as they race against time to overcome technical disasters, network pressure, and backstage chaos before going live.

Trivia

  • The film dramatizes the events before the debut episode of Saturday Night Live.
  • Jason Reitman co-wrote the screenplay after extensive interviews with original cast and crew members.
  • The ensemble cast portrays many of the original SNL performers and creators.
