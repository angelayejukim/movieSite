Overview

The Phoenician Scheme follows wealthy businessman Zsa-zsa Korda as he embarks on a dangerous mission to secure his ambitious financial empire. Joined by his estranged daughter, Sister Liesl, and an eccentric tutor, the trio navigates assassination attempts, political intrigue, and unexpected family bonds in Wes Anderson's signature whimsical style.
