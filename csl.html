Overview

Crazy, Stupid, Love. follows Cal Weaver, whose seemingly perfect life is turned upside down after his wife asks for a divorce. With help from the charismatic Jacob Palmer, Cal learns to navigate modern dating while multiple interconnected love stories reveal that relationships are often messy, unexpected, and deeply rewarding.

Trivia

  • The film features an ensemble cast including Steve Carell, Ryan Gosling, Emma Stone, Julianne Moore, and Kevin Bacon.
  • Ryan Gosling and Emma Stone's chemistry in the film led to them starring together again in Gangster Squad and La La Land.
