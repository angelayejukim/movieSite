Overview

Jane Nichols has been a bridesmaid 27 times but has never been a bride. When her younger sister captures the heart of the man Jane secretly loves, she begins to question whether she's spent too much of her life putting everyone else's happiness before her own.

Trivia

  • The film's iconic bridesmaid dress montage became one of its most memorable scenes.
  • Katherine Heigl performed many of the comedic scenes herself.
  • The movie has become a popular romantic comedy comfort film.
