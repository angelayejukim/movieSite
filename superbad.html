Overview

Superbad follows best friends Seth and Evan as they set out on one unforgettable night before graduating high school. Their mission to score alcohol for a party quickly spirals into a series of outrageous mishaps, testing their friendship and forcing them to face the uncertainty of growing up.

Trivia

  • The screenplay was written by Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg, who based many events on their own teenage experiences.
  • Christopher Mintz-Plasse's fake ID character, "McLovin," became one of the most iconic comedy characters of the 2000s.
