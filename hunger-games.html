Overview

The Hunger Games takes place in a dystopian future where the nation of Panem forces 24 teenagers from its districts to compete in a televised fight to the death called the Hunger Games. Katniss Everdeen volunteers to take her sister's place and must rely on survival skills, intelligence, and courage to stay alive in the arena.

Trivia

  • The film is based on the novel by Suzanne Collins.
  • The movie was a massive box office success and launched a four-film series.
