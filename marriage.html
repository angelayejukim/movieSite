Overview

A stage director and an actress navigate a painful coast-to-coast divorce that pushes them to their emotional limits while trying to remain devoted parents to their young son. Through heartbreak, conflict, and compassion, they discover that love can endure even when a marriage cannot.

Trivia

  • Laura Dern won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress for her performance.
  • Noah Baumbach drew inspiration from his own experiences while writing the screenplay.
  • Adam Driver's emotional argument scene was filmed over multiple days with minimal cuts.
