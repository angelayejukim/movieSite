Overview

Scott Pilgrim vs. the World follows Scott Pilgrim, a bass guitarist in a garage band, who falls in love with the mysterious Ramona Flowers. To win her heart, he must battle and defeat her seven evil exes in a series of over-the-top, video game-inspired fights.

Trivia

  • The film is based on Bryan Lee O'Malley's graphic novel series.
  • Its visual style combines comic book effects with classic video game references.
  • Although modest at the box office, it became a major cult classic.
