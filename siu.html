Overview

Set It Up follows two overworked assistants, Harper and Charlie, who devise a plan to set up their demanding bosses in hopes of getting some free time. As their matchmaking scheme unfolds, they unexpectedly develop feelings for each other, discovering that love can appear when least expected.

Trivia

  • The film premiered on Netflix in 2018 and became one of its most popular romantic comedies.
  • Zoey Deutch and Glen Powell received praise for their on-screen chemistry.
  • The movie helped revive interest in classic romantic comedy films.
