Overview

The Intern follows Ben Whittaker, a 70-year-old widower who joins an online fashion startup as a senior intern. As he forms an unexpected friendship with the company's busy CEO, Jules Ostin, they both learn valuable lessons about work, life, and personal growth.

Trivia

  • Robert De Niro and Anne Hathaway starred together for the first time in this film.
  • In one scene, Ben is confused about who Jay-Z is, a humorous reference that became one of the film's memorable moments.
