Overview

Priscilla chronicles the life of Priscilla Presley, tracing her relationship with Elvis Presley from their first meeting through the challenges of life inside Graceland. Told from Priscilla's perspective, the film explores identity, love, fame, and the search for independence.

Trivia

  • The film is based on Priscilla Presley's 1985 memoir Elvis and Me.
  • Cailee Spaeny won the Volpi Cup for Best Actress at the 2023 Venice International Film Festival for her performance.
