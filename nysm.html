Overview

Now You See Me follows a group of talented illusionists known as "The Four Horsemen" who perform spectacular magic shows while secretly pulling off daring bank heists. As an FBI agent and an Interpol detective pursue them, the line between illusion and reality becomes increasingly blurred.

Trivia

  • The film combines stage magic with crime and thriller elements.
  • The cast trained with professional magicians to make the illusion performances look authentic.
