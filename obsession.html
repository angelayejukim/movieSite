Overview

Obsession follows Bear, a lonely young man who makes a wish for his longtime crush, Nikki, to finally fall in love with him. When the wish comes true through a mysterious supernatural force, Bear soon discovers that getting exactly what he wanted comes at a terrifying cost, leading to deadly consequences and forcing him to confront the true nature of obsession.

Trivia

  • Obsession is Curry Barker's feature film debut after gaining a following on YouTube for his horror shorts and sketches.
  • It was made for under $1 million and was acquired by Focus Features after a multimillion-dollar bidding war following its TIFF premiere.
