Overview

Companion follows a young couple whose weekend getaway with friends takes a shocking turn after hidden truths are revealed. As trust unravels and technology blurs the line between human and artificial intelligence, everyone must fight to survive.

Trivia

  • The film marks the feature directorial debut of Drew Hancock.
  • It blends psychological thriller, dark comedy, and science fiction while keeping many of its biggest twists hidden in early marketing.
