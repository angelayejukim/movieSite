Overview

Kingsman: The Secret Service follows Gary "Eggsy" Unwin, a talented but troubled young man who is recruited into a secret British spy agency. Under the guidance of Harry Hart, Eggsy trains to become a gentleman spy while confronting the tech billionaire Richmond Valentine, whose plan threatens the future of humanity.

Trivia

  • The film is based on the comic book The Secret Service by Mark Millar and Dave Gibbons.
  • The church fight scene became one of the most iconic action sequences in modern cinema.
  • "Manners maketh man" became the franchise's signature quote.
