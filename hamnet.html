Overview

A historical drama about William Shakespeare and his wife Agnes as they deal with the loss of their son Hamnet, exploring grief and the inspiration behind Shakespeare's play Hamlet.

Trivia

  • The movie is based on the novel Hamnet by Maggie O'Farrell.
  • The story focuses on Shakespeare's family rather than his plays.
