Overview

Toy Story follows Woody, a loyal cowboy doll whose place as Andy's favorite toy is threatened by the arrival of the space ranger Buzz Lightyear. Their rivalry soon turns into an unexpected friendship as the two must work together to find their way back home before Andy's family moves away.

Trivia

  • Toy Story was the first feature-length film created entirely with computer-generated animation.
  • It launched Pixar Animation Studios as a major force in filmmaking and became one of the most influential animated films of all time.
