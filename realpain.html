Overview

A Real Pain follows cousins David and Benji as they travel through Poland to honor their late grandmother. What begins as a heritage tour soon becomes an emotional journey as old tensions, family history, and personal grief resurface, forcing the pair to reconnect in unexpected ways.

Trivia

  • Jesse Eisenberg wrote, directed, and co-starred in the film.
  • Kieran Culkin received widespread acclaim for his performance, earning multiple major acting awards during the 2024–25 awards season.
